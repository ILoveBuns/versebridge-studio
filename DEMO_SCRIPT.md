# 90-second demo script

## 0:00–0:12 — Problem

“AI makes content creation fast, but spiritual content has a trust problem:
models can misquote Scripture or blur the source with generated commentary.”

## 0:12–0:25 — Product

“VerseBridge Studio turns a passage into a creator-ready content pack while
keeping licensed Scripture and AI commentary visibly separate.”

## 0:25–0:52 — Live flow

1. Enter `JHN.3.16` and a YouVersion Bible ID.
2. Choose “young adults navigating uncertainty.”
3. Select “Instagram carousel” and “hopeful and grounded.”
4. Click **Create grounded pack**.

“The passage comes from YouVersion. Only this grounded source and the creator
brief go to Gloo AI.”

## 0:52–1:14 — Result

“The result contains a headline, caption, reflection prompt, and visual
direction. The reference and source link stay attached. Most importantly,
verified Scripture and generated commentary have different labels and visual
treatment.”

## 1:14–1:30 — Frontier

“The same structured pack can power social posts, podcasts, newsletters,
wearables, and ambient displays. VerseBridge makes Scripture-native creation
faster without making the source less trustworthy.”
